Data quality

What had to be cleaned before any of the numbers above could be trusted. These are issues in the source sheets, shown rather than hidden.

How duplicates are resolved

  • The WA and CRM workbooks both hold the same bookings, so every order is collapsed to one record keyed on order number before anything is counted.
  • When one order carries several statuses, the furthest-along wins: Completed > In Progress > Pending > Cancelled. An order once marked complete did complete; a later "Pending" row is a stale export.
  • Phone numbers arrive as integers, floats and quoted text. All are normalised to 10 digits before joining.

How the join works

  • Phone is the only usable key — Customer ID is empty for all 13,809 CRM follow-up rows.
  • Numbers arrive as integers, floats (9971372011.0) and text ('9310311589). All are normalised to 10 digits, stripping +91.
  • Validated against the WA Marketing tabs: 333/333, 214/214 and 510/510 phones matched exactly.

Data issues worth fixing

  • 3,560 CRM registrations have the phone column formatted as a date. Excel exports read them as blank; reading raw values recovers 707 extra customers.
  • 47 of 106 members have no registration record — they were signed up at the salon, never in the app.
  • The CRM sheet adds a new Remarks DD-MM-YYYY column pair every day; it is 44 columns wide and growing.
  • The N Time Users tabs are snapshots and have drifted from live bookings.
